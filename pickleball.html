On court

Everyone can see the score

Prop the phone against the fence and the score turns into a board with digits big enough to read from the far baseline. No more “what was it?” three rallies later, no more arguing about whose serve it is.

Open play

Your rotation runs itself

Set up a mixer for four to sixteen players and the app builds every round: who plays whom, on which court, who sits out. Standings update as games finish. One organiser, a whole session that runs on rails.

After the game

See who actually won the rallies

Tag a point in one tap and you find out what the score never tells you: your winners, your unforced errors, the ones your opponents handed over. With heart rate on top, so you know what the session really cost.

How it works

Three taps and you are playing.

Pick pickleball on the watch

Sport lives on your wrist, so you never dig the phone out of the bag. Side-out is the default; switch to rally scoring if that is how your club plays.

Score with your thumb

Two big zones, us and them. Tap between serves, with a sweaty hand, in a glove. Undo is one tap away when someone calls it back.

Send the game to your partner

One link after the game. They open it and see it from their own side — even without the app installed, and it lands in their history if they have it.

What it does not do — yet

  • No DUPR sync. Your rating there is not affected, and we are not pretending otherwise.
  • No partner watch sync. One watch scores the game; everyone else reads the phone on the fence.
  • It will not find you a court or a game. Use whatever app your club already uses for that.
  • Apple Watch does the scoring. Without one you can still receive and read games your partners send you.
